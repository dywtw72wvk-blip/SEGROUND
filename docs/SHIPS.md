# Fleet ships: FS Carrier Atmo, FS Buster Atmo, and the full set

Three ready-to-load blueprints for planetary (atmospheric) flight. The Fleet System scripts are **already inside** every programmable block, and every setting has a working default, so nothing needs configuring.

| Blueprint | What you get |
|-----------|--------------|
| **FS Carrier Atmo + 8 Busters** | **The whole fleet in one paste.** The carrier with a Buster docked at all 8 flank connectors. |
| FS Carrier Atmo | The carrier on its own |
| FS Buster Atmo | One Buster, for adding more, or for loading into a `[Factory]` projector |

## One paste, ready to fly (FS Carrier Atmo + 8 Busters)

1. Press F10, choose **FS Carrier Atmo + 8 Busters** and paste it.
2. Load uranium into the carrier's 4 reactors. Inventory can't travel in a blueprint.

Then, with no input from you:
1. **About 2 s:** the scripts start. Hub Core locks every dock that has a Buster in it. This is a backup; the blueprint already records the connectors as connected.
2. **Within about 10 s:** the hub hands each docked Buster the network key. They register as `BUSTER-01` to `BUSTER-08` and show `DOCKED` on the cockpit's fleet screen while they charge from the carrier.
3. **Once charged to 80%:** the defense manager launches 2 Busters to escort the carrier (alert GREEN). It sends more if threats appear, and rotates them through charging.

Want them all to stay docked? Run `defense off`. Want them all out? Run `scramble`.

How the docked positions are built: each Buster sits nose-in, upright, with its connector face 5 cm from the carrier connector's face. That's inside the game's 0.35 m connect range. The generator checks that the connector faces line up and that no Buster block touches the carrier or another Buster.

They're generated by `tools/shipgen.py` from block data on the Space Engineers wiki: size, mass and thrust for every block. `install.bat` then checks each block against your own game's definition files before installing:
- the block ID exists,
- it's on the right grid size,
- no blocks overlap.

Anything that fails the check isn't installed, and the installer lists the problems.

## Getting them in game

1. Run `install.bat`. It copies both blueprints into `%AppData%\SpaceEngineers\Blueprints\local`.
2. In game, press **F10**, open the **Local** blueprints tab and pick the ship.
   - **Creative:** the ship goes to your clipboard; place it.
   - **Survival:** load it into a projector and weld it.

## FS Buster Atmo (small grid defense drone)

| | |
|---|---|
| Size | 5.5 × 7.5 × 7.0 m (W × H × L), 255 blocks |
| Mass | 17.7 t (18.0 t with ammo) |
| Lift | 1 large atmospheric thruster, sitting on the centre of mass. **Thrust-to-weight 3.0** at sea level in 1 g, so it keeps a healthy margin as the air thins. |
| Horizontal | 6 small atmospheric thrusters: 9.8 m/s² forward and back, 4.9 m/s² sideways. No down thrusters; gravity does that, and the flight script accounts for it. |
| Weapons | 2 Gatling turrets, top and belly, covering the whole sphere. They're always active (not tagged `[Combat]`), so a Buster defends itself and reports contacts to the hub, which raises the alert. |
| Power | 2 batteries |
| Control | Remote control (forward = nose), 2 gyroscopes, programmable block `[Fleet Drone]` running the Drone script, antenna at 3 km |
| Docking | Nose connector (small-grid regular connector), which mates with a large-grid connector |
| Factory | A merge-block fin under the nose for factory printing. Both of its side faces are open, so either can mate. |

**First flight:**
1. Paste a Buster with its nose connector touching one of the carrier's `[Dock]` connectors, then lock it.
2. Within about 10 seconds the hub provisions it (network ID and key) and it shows up as `BUSTER-xx` on the fleet screen.

## FS Carrier Atmo (large grid drone carrier)

| | |
|---|---|
| Size | 27.5 × 30 × 70 m (W × H × L; the height includes the antenna mast), 586 blocks |
| Mass | 607 t empty, about 748 t with 8 Busters docked |
| Lift | 2 large and 8 small atmospheric thrusters. **Thrust-to-weight 2.74** empty, **2.22** fully loaded. |
| Drive | 1 large atmospheric thruster forward and 1 back (9.6 m/s²), plus 3 small atmospheric thrusters per side (2.9 m/s²) |
| Power | 4 small reactors (60 MW) and 4 batteries. **Load the reactors with uranium.** Hovering draws about 15 MW, and charging drones adds more. |
| Control | Cockpit at the bow, which also sets the hub's forward direction. 6 gyroscopes. Programmable blocks `[Fleet Core]` and `[Fleet UI]`. |
| Screens | The cockpit's built-in screens show the fleet list (screen 0), tactical map (1) and hangar (2). If they land on the wrong screens, rename the cockpit's `[FS:...:n]` tags. |
| Docks | **8 `[Dock]` connectors**, 4 per side on the deck edges, facing outward and 12.5 m apart. Busters dock nose-in from the flanks, so keep about 25 m of open air beside the carrier when recovering drones. |
| Defense | 2 Gatling turrets on the deck. A turret target raises the hub to RED. Antenna at 5 km. |

**After pasting:**
1. Load uranium into the 4 reactors.
2. Open `[Fleet Core]`'s Custom Data and change `Key=change-me`.
3. Paste Busters docked at the flank connectors, or fly them in.
4. Run `escort all` and fly.

**Not included (add them if you want them):**
- **Jump drive.** This is a planetary design.
- **Conveyor network.** Reactors and drone ammo are topped up by hand. For automatic rearming, add conveyors and a `[Rearm]` sorter.
- **Landing gear.**

## What isn't verified until you load it

- **Block IDs, grid sizes and overlaps** are verified on your PC by `install.bat` against your game's own definition files.
- **Flight numbers** (mass, thrust-to-weight, centre of mass, clear exhaust paths) are computed and checked by the generator.
- **Not yet confirmed in game:**
  - whether the cockpit's screen numbering matches the tags,
  - whether the Busters' small-grid connector physically mates with the carrier's large-grid connector,
  - how far the thruster flames actually reach.

  The generator keeps 3 m clear behind Buster thrusters and 10 m behind carrier thrusters.

Report anything odd. Ship designs live in `tools/shipgen.py`; edit the layouts there and run `python3 tools/build.py`.
